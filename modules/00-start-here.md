# Module 00 — Start Here & Safety

[← Home](../index.md) | [Next: System Baseline →](01-system-baseline.md)

## Goal

Preserve the current system long enough to collect useful evidence without creating new variables.

## Current Hardware Rule

The machine currently has the **Ryzen 7 5800X installed**.

The **Ryzen 9 5900X is already removed**.

Until remote support says otherwise:

- Do not swap CPUs again.
- Do not move RAM between slots.
- Do not reinstall Windows.
- Do not reset or flash the BIOS.
- Do not run DDU.
- Do not replace the GPU.
- Do not unplug internal cables "just to see if it helps."
- Do not change PBO, Curve Optimizer, RAM timings, voltages, overclocks, or undervolts.

Changing multiple things at once can make the problem harder to identify.

## Normal Use Is Allowed

Continue using the PC for normal activities such as:

- Marvel Rivals
- PEAK
- other multiplayer/co-op games
- Discord
- OBS
- VTube Studio / tracking
- drawing software and tablet
- browser/video
- normal streaming accessories

A failure during normal use is valuable because it tells us what the machine does during the actual workload.

# Safety Stop Conditions

Immediately shut down and unplug the PC if there is:

- smoke
- burning-electronics smell
- visible sparks or arcing
- melted/discolored power connectors
- repeated PSU clicking
- a fan physically jammed
- a new harsh electrical buzzing sound
- repeated automatic on/off cycling every few seconds

Do not repeatedly power the machine back on after one of these symptoms.

A normal black screen, by itself, does **not** require unplugging the PC.

# Troubleshooting Discipline

Use this sequence:

```text
Observe
   ↓
Record
   ↓
Collect logs
   ↓
Review evidence
   ↓
Choose one next test
```

Avoid:

```text
Crash
   ↓
Random driver update
   ↓
Move RAM
   ↓
Reset BIOS
   ↓
Reinstall something
   ↓
Now nobody knows which change mattered
```
